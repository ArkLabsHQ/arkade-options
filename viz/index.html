What the scripts check

  • Finalize: side is 0 or 1; collateral and premium are each greater than 330; the deadline has not been reached.
  • Side 0: the intent input is at least collateral; output 0 pays the writer program at least premium plus any sats above collateral; output 1 pays the vault program at least collateral.
  • Settle: one input. Kind is 0 or 1. Strike is 1 through 1,000,000,000. Collateral is 331 through 1,000,000,000. Expiry is above 1,800.
  • Nine oracle checks, three distinct oracles per slice. Each price is 1 through 1,000,000,000, each time is positive, and the message is sha256 of BTCUSD, the price, and the time. The slice max must sit in [expiry−1,800, expiry−1,740], [expiry−960, expiry−900], or [expiry, expiry+60], and the three times in a slice span at most 60 seconds.
  • TWAP is the median of each slice, then (m0×900 + m1×900 + m2×60) / 1,860. Covered call: if ST is above K, the holder leg is Q×(ST−K)/ST, truncating. A leg of 330 or less is not its own output.
  • Side 0 change has no dust test. Sats above collateral are added to the premium output, including a 1-sat extra.

Swap and SingleSig

NonInteractiveSwap is the pattern OptionIntent copies. The desk does not send it. bindSwap is used by the program check and the e2e script’s address print. Asset amounts here are that program check: offer 1,000, want 2,000. They are asset units. The script does not pin the BTC on those outputs, so this picture does not invent a sat split.
